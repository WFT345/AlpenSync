AlpenSync is an open-source Proton contacts sync app for GrapheneOS and other de-Googled Android (LineageOS and similar).

<b>Contacts ready, still in development.</b> Two-way Proton contact sync works: pull into the stock Contacts app and dialer, push local creates, edits, and deletes back to Proton, with a persistent outbox if the network drops. A first pull has been verified on GrapheneOS. Calendar and mail come later, same core, still without Google Play Services.

<b>What AlpenSync will never do:</b>

* No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no trackers of any kind
* No network traffic to anything except Proton's servers — enforced in the app configuration and auditable in the source
* All cryptography happens on your device; the app is a client, never a relay

<b>Honest limitations:</b>

* AlpenSync uses Proton's undocumented, unofficial API. Sync may break without notice when Proton changes something.
* Your contacts are stored decrypted in Android's standard system providers — that is how any sync app makes them visible to the dialer.
* Two-password Proton accounts are not supported yet. TOTP 2FA is supported; hardware security keys are not.
